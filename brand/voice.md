# LedgerFlow AI brand voice

LedgerFlow AI is a made-up practice brand. It is a B2B accounting automation studio. We help small business owners, accounting firms and finance managers improve their bookkeeping workflows, verify bank statements, and automate repetitive QuickBooks and Excel work.

Our services are **Accounting Workflow Automation** and **AI-Assisted Finance Automation**. We call our customers "clients" or "your finance team".

**Core principle:** Evidence before claims, privacy by design, open about our limits, and people stay accountable. Never promise more than our software has actually shown it can do.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Precise, Trustworthy, Human-supervised.**

- **Precise:** Say exactly what we do and what we checked. Use real numbers only when we have them.
- **Trustworthy:** Be calm, open and evidence-based. Say what our limits are.
- **Human-supervised:** Make it clear that the client's team reviews the results and makes the decisions.

What it sounds like:

> "We automate repetitive accounting tasks, verify the results, and keep your finance team in control of every important decision."

Tone: calm, clear, respectful and professional. Be concise and approachable. Write in plain English and explain any technical term in simple words. Focus on results and checks we can measure, not AI hype.

## 2. Words we use

- Reconcile, verified, accurate, traceable, transparent
- Bank reconciliation, financial records, QuickBooks, Excel automation
- Human-reviewed, exception reports, audit trail, data privacy
- Clients, your finance team
- Accounting Workflow Automation, AI-Assisted Finance Automation

Phrases we use:

- "Your team stays in control."
- "Every transaction can be traced back to its source."
- "Automate repetitive work, not financial judgment."

## 3. Words we avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge | Practical, reliable, tested |
| AI magic, one-click accounting | Automated workflows with verification |
| 100% accurate, zero errors, guaranteed results | Tested, verified against defined checks, with exceptions reported |
| Replace accountants, fully autonomous finance | Human-supervised automation |
| Instant reconciliation | Faster reconciliation with review |
| FBR-approved, tax-compliant by default | Compliance support, subject to professional review and applicable regulations |
| Military-grade security, unhackable | Privacy-conscious, with clearly documented safeguards |
| Save 90% of your time | Measurable efficiency improvements, supported by actual results |

Tones we avoid:

- Pushy sales language and promises we can't back up
- Technical jargon we don't need
- Writing that is too casual
- Marketing that tries to scare people
- Criticising accountants

## 4. Claims we must never make

1. **Replacing accountants.** Never describe our services as replacing accountants.
2. **Partnerships or approvals.** Never claim a partnership, certification, endorsement or approval from Intuit, QuickBooks, Microsoft, FBR, SRB or any other organisation without documented evidence.
3. **Client details.** Never name clients, reveal financial records, publish testimonials or share project details without explicit permission.
4. **Made-up results.** Never invent performance figures, time savings, accuracy rates or success stories. Use only results that have been checked independently, and say what they cover and where they stop.
5. **Absolute data claims.** Never say client data always stays local, is never seen by third parties, or is completely secure, unless that system's design and safeguards back it up.
6. **Tax, legal or audit promises.** Never promise tax compliance, guaranteed deductions, audit certification or legally binding financial advice. Questions about regulations need review by a qualified professional.
7. **AI acting alone.** Never claim AI can approve payments, finalise accounts, submit tax returns or make important financial decisions without proper human approval.
8. **Unfinished features.** Never present prototypes, experiments or planned integrations as if they were ready to use.
9. **Works with everything.** Never claim the software works with every version of QuickBooks, Excel, banks or accounting systems unless we have tested those versions and workflows.

Every financial output must be reviewable, traceable and subject to the right human approval.

## 5. How we sign off

```
Kind regards,

The LedgerFlow AI Team
Accounting Workflow Automation

Verified workflows. Human decisions.
```

Every marketing email footer must:

- include a clear unsubscribe link,
- say why the reader is getting the email (for example: "You're receiving this because you joined the LedgerFlow AI waitlist."),
- include a business address placeholder, `[Business address]`, until we have a real address.

Never invent contact details, certifications or company registration details. This is a practice brand, so use made-up contact details and placeholders, and never send real marketing emails.
