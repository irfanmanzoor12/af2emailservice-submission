# Email Studio

This studio is where we plan and write email campaigns for a small business. We work out who to email, what to say and when to send it, then write the emails in the brand's voice.

## Folders

- `brand/`: how the brand sounds, including its voice, the words it uses and the claims it must never make.
- `leads/`: the practice customer list we write and send to.
- `campaigns/`: one folder per campaign, with its plan and its emails.
- `notes/`: research, decisions and scratch work that isn't part of a campaign.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words, with no jargon.
- Never send email to real people. When sending through Resend, send only to Resend's test addresses (ending in `@resend.dev`) and to my own email address. A guard will enforce this in the next step.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
