# Handoff: email 01 subject line

- Small business owners: fewer hours matching bank statements by hand, books they can trust, no new tools to learn.
- Accounting firms: the same reconciliation steps across every client, and an audit trail to show clients at busy month and year end.
- Finance managers: a faster month-end close, traceable records, and exception reports, with the team keeping approvals.
- Angle A (notes/angle-a.md): accurate, traceable bank reconciliation and human review. Best line: "Now open: every match traced to its source".
- Angle B (notes/angle-b.md): less repetitive work, with the team still in control. Best line: "Less repetitive work. Your team stays in control."
- Pick: Angle B. All three groups' biggest pain is repetitive work, and B speaks to it while keeping the "people decide" promise.
- B also uses the brand's own phrases and makes no accuracy or time claims. Angle A's "Verified bank matches" is close to an accuracy promise.
- Keep Angle A's traceability for the email body and for finance managers and firms in later emails.
